Our Heritage

The Art of
Invisible Luxury

LUXORÉ was not built. It was composed — note by note, year by year — by perfumers who believed that the greatest luxury is one you cannot see.

The Origin

From Grasse,
with Love

LUXORÉ was born in 2026 in India, the perfume capital of the world. Our founder, a perfumer, returned from years of study in the ateliers of Parisian haute parfumerie with one ambition: to create fragrances that felt genuinely rare.

We work with fewer than 20 ingredient houses worldwide, sourcing only what meets our uncompromising standards — Bulgarian rose absolute, Laotian oud, Indian sandalwood, Haitian vetiver.

Each fragrance takes between 18 months and 4 years to develop. There is no shortcut to beauty.

The Difference

Our Pillars

Time & Patience

We do not rush. Our fragrances mature in our laboratory for months before they are approved. Excellence is never hasty.

Rare Ingredients

From wild-harvested ambergris to ethically sourced Indian sandalwood — we use only what nature's finest terroirs have to offer.

Master Composition

Every LUXORÉ fragrance is the work of a named master perfumer, with decades of expertise and an unwavering artistic vision.
